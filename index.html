One place for every classroom at LPU

See which rooms are free right now, book makeup classes when you're on leave, and let admins manage timetables, rooms and users from a single dashboard.

Sign in See what it does

Live free-rooms grid

Real-time view of every room and time slot, so finding an empty classroom takes seconds.

Makeup from leave

Faculty on leave can book a replacement slot straight from the room they need.

CSV timetable import

Admins upload the semester timetable in bulk instead of entering it row by row.

Assignment collection

Create a share link and students submit files with just their ID. No student accounts needed.

Install as an app

Works on phones and desktops as an installable PWA, with an offline indicator.

Secure by default

Role-based access, bot protection on login and forced password change on first sign-in.

Who is it for?

Faculty

  • Check free rooms
  • Book and manage your classes
  • Create assignment links and review submissions

Administrators

  • Manage users, rooms, slots and the calendar
  • Import and edit timetables
  • Oversee bookings and assignments
